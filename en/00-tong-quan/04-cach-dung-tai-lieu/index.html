How to use these materials

Who these materials are for

  • Teachers: lesson plans for preparing and leading each session.
  • Students: read before the lesson, review afterwards, do the homework.
  • Parents: follow what their child is learning and what support is needed at home.

Folder structure

  • Each section (level, guide, appendix) is a main entry on the home page.
  • Each lesson is one page; the order follows the number at the start of the name (01, 02, 03…).
  • Example path: /en/02-cap-1-nhap-mon/03-len-day/ → Level 1, Lesson 3: Tuning.

Structure of a lesson

  • Objectives: what the student can do after the lesson.
  • Preparation: what teacher and student need before the lesson.
  • Main content: the knowledge and technique to teach.
  • Lesson flow (60 minutes): a suggested time split for each part.
  • Online teaching notes: camera angles, audio, how to demonstrate over the internet.
  • Common mistakes: mistakes students often make and how to fix them.
  • Homework: what to practise and which video to submit.
  • Completion criteria: a checklist before moving on to the next lesson.
  • Media: illustrative images, videos, audio and PDF files.

What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
chungCore — every student learns it
co-dienClassical track
dem-hatAccompaniment track (đệm hát / contemporary)
draftDraft, content still changing
reviewBeing checked by a teacher
readyComplete, ready to teach
  • Students only need the chung lessons and the lessons of the track they chose.

The "Media cần bổ sung" box

  • A yellow box marked "Media cần bổ sung" (media still needed) means the image / video has not been recorded yet; the caption describes what to record.
  • Once the file is added to the assets/ folder, the yellow box automatically becomes the real image / video.
  • Teachers use this list as a "shot list" for recording sessions.

Tips for use

  • Teachers: read the lesson 1 day before teaching; prepare demonstrations and backing tracks if needed.
  • Teachers: you do not have to cover every bullet point — adapt to the student's pace.
  • Students: read the "Objectives" and watch the video before the lesson (5–10 minutes).
  • Students: after the lesson, use the "Completion criteria" to check yourself before recording your homework video.
  • Unfamiliar terms: look them up in the Vietnamese – English glossary.

The English edition

  • Vietnamese is the primary language; the English edition lives under /en/ with the same paths.
  • Pages not yet translated show the Vietnamese version with a "translation in progress" note.

Updating the materials (for teachers / editors)

  • Writing rules and file structure: see docs/CONTENT_GUIDE.md.
  • Preview the site: cargo run -- serve, then open http://127.0.0.1:8080.
  • Create a new lesson: cargo run -- new vi/<folder>/<number>-<lesson-name> "Title".
  • List missing media and pages still in draft: cargo run -- report.
  • Never put song lyrics or copied tabs of copyrighted songs in the materials; song titles + chord progressions are fine.
