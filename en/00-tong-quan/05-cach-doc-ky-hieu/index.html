How to read guitar notation

Draft

Reading tab, chord diagrams, strum patterns and fretboard maps used throughout the curriculum.

123456
On this page
  • Objectives
  • Tab (tablature)
  • Chord diagrams
  • Strum patterns
  • Fretboard maps

Objectives

  • Understand the 4 kinds of illustration used in every lesson: tab, chord diagrams, strum patterns, fretboard maps.
  • Read homework on your own without the teacher explaining it again.

Tab (tablature)

  • 6 lines = 6 strings; the top line is string 1 (high e — thinnest), the bottom is string 6 (low E — thickest).
  • A number = the fret to press; 0 = open string. Read left to right.
  • Vertical bars | = bar lines. Letters under the staff = right-hand fingers (p i m a).
  • Technique marks: h hammer-on, p pull-off, / \ slide, <12> harmonic.

Chord diagrams

  • Vertical view: 6 vertical lines = 6 strings (left = string 6, low E; right = string 1, high e); horizontal lines = frets.
  • The thick bar on top = the nut; a label like 5fr means the diagram starts at fret 5.
  • Dots = where to press, the number inside = left-hand finger (1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 little, T thumb).
  • ○ above = play the open string; × = don't play that string.
  • A long bar across several strings = barre.
  • Text form x32010 reads string 6 → string 1; in lessons it always comes with a mini diagram, e.g. C = x32010.
Example: open and barre chords

Strum patterns

  • Each cell = half a beat (eighth note) in 4/4; the number above is how you count: 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &.
  • ↓ strum down, ↑ strum up; · keep the hand moving but miss the strings.
  • B = pick the bass note, B' = second bass note (alternating bass); an arrow with × = muted "chop" strum.
  • > above the count = accent.
  • Press ▶ to hear the pattern (played on an E minor chord); press again to stop.

Fretboard maps

  • The neck is horizontal, as you see it looking down at the guitar: string 1 (e) on top, fret numbers underneath.
  • Dots = notes to play; the number inside = finger (or note name); red dot = root note.
  • Hollow circles left of the nut = open strings.

Practice tools on every page

  • Bottom right: 🥁 Metronome (BPM, beats per bar, tap tempo) and 🎵 Tuner (reference pitch for each string).
  • At the end of each lesson: "Mark this lesson as done" — progress shows on the fret strip at the top of the lesson and in the menu.
  • The ☐ boxes under "Completion criteria" are clickable for self-checking; your browser remembers them.
