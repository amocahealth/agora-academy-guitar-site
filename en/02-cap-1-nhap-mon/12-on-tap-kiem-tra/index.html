Level 1: Beginner · Lesson 12/17

Lesson 12: Level 1 review and assessment

Core⏱ 60 minDraft

Review of all 11 lessons, assessment with a shared rubric plus a track-specific part, advice on moving to Level 2.

Objectives

  • Students demonstrate their Level 1 skills in a clearly structured assessment.
  • The teacher decides whether the student Passes / Passes conditionally / Has not yet passed and chooses the track for Level 2.
  • Students receive specific feedback: 2 strengths, 2 areas to improve.

Preparation

  • Teacher: assessment sheet (table below) as a Google Sheet or on paper; a list of quick questions; review the videos the student sent for lessons 10 and 11.
  • Student: guitar in tune, metronome, the accompaniment/melody videos already sent; tell the teacher in advance which song or melody they will perform.

Main content

  • Quick review (15'): posture → tuning → 5 chords → chord progression → track piece.
  • The assessment has 2 parts:
    • Core part (items 1–7 in the rubric) — every student.
    • Track part (item 8) — Accompaniment: accompany a whole song; Classical: Ode to Joy + Lightly Row.
  • Students may submit the track part as a pre-recorded video (encouraged — better audio than live).

Assessment rubric (each item 0–2 points)

#Skill0 — Not yet1 — Partly achieved2 — Achieved
1PostureTense shoulders/wrists, guitar unstableCorrect but needs remindersGets into correct, relaxed posture unprompted
2TuningCan't tune on their ownCan tune, > 3 minutes≤ 3 minutes, all 6 strings correct
3String names, reading tabMany errors≥ 3/5 questions correct5/5 questions correct
4Right hand (p, i–m, pick)No alternation, jerky handAlternates but unevenlyEven and relaxed
55 chords Em, Am, C, G, D< 3 chords ring clearly3–4 chords ring clearlyAll 5 ring clearly, correct x/o
6Chord changesStops > 2 beatsStops 1–2 beats4-chord progression, tempo 60, no stopping
7RhythmCan't keep the beatKeeps the beat but speeds up/slows downLocked to the metronome, counts aloud correctly
8Track pieceDoesn't finish the pieceFinishes, stopping 2–3 timesWhole piece, no stops, in time
  • Maximum total: 16.
    • ≥ 12 with no item at 0: Pass — move up to Level 2.
    • 9–11 (at most one item at 0), or ≥ 12 with exactly one item at 0: Conditional pass — move up to Level 2 with 1–2 extra sessions on weak skills.
    • ≤ 8: Not yet passed — review for 2–4 sessions on weak skills, then reassess.
    • Two or more items at 0 (even with a total ≥ 9 or ≥ 12): Not yet passed — 1–2 review sessions focused on the 0-point items, then reassess just those items (scores on the other items are kept).
  • Suggested quick questions (item 3): "What is string 3 called?", "What does a 3 on the 2nd line of tab mean?", "What does x mean on a chord diagram?", "Which string is not played on Am?", "How many beats per bar in 3/4?".

Lesson flow (60 minutes)

  • 5' — Warm-up, tuning (score item 2 at the same time)
  • 15' — Quick review of everything, reassure the student
  • 25' — Assess items 1–8 using the rubric
  • 10' — Feedback: 2 strengths, 2 areas to improve; advice on the Level 2 track
  • 5' — Send the assessment sheet and a practice plan for the next stage

Online teaching notes

  • Before the assessment: re-check the full-body camera + hand close-up camera, noise suppression off, student's guitar in tune (tuner app read on camera).
  • Don't score items 6, 7, 8 from live audio alone if the connection is unstable → use a pre-recorded video or ask for a recording after the session.
  • The student runs the metronome on their own device; the teacher never plays along.
  • Fill in the scores during the session and screen-share the sheet at the end for the student (and the parent, for children).
  • If the connection drops mid-assessment: no penalty; let the student redo that item.
  • Save the assessment video in the student's profile — compare progress at the end of Level 2.

Common mistakes

  • Nervous students play faster than usual → remind them to count in one bar before starting.
  • The teacher marks sound quality too harshly, mistaking network compression for buzzing.
  • Skipping positive feedback → the student loses motivation.

Homework

  • Follow the personal practice plan the teacher sends after the session (focusing on items scored ≤ 1).
  • Keep up 20–30 minutes/day while waiting for Level 2.
  • Accompaniment track: choose 2 songs to learn in Level 2; Classical track: listen to one classical guitar piece each week.

Completion criteria

  • Total score ≥ 12/16 with no item at 0.
  • Has received the assessment sheet and practice plan.
  • Has confirmed the Level 2 track (Classical / Accompaniment).
