Level 2: Foundation · Lesson 3/12

Lesson 3: Notes on Strings 4, 5, 6

Classical track⏱ 60 minDraft

First-position notes on the three bass strings, ledger lines below the staff and reading bass melodies.

Objectives

  • Students find the first-position notes on strings 4, 5 and 6.
  • Read the ledger-line notes below the staff (C, B, A, G, F, E).
  • Play a melody spread across all 6 strings, plucking the bass with the thumb (p).

Preparation

  • Teacher: PDF chart of notes on strings 4–6, a "complete first position" PDF (6 strings), a short bass melody.
  • Student: guitar, metronome, the PDF.

Main content

  • String 4 (D): D (open, below line 1), E (fret 2, line 1), F (fret 3, space 1).
  • String 5 (A): A (open, ledger line 2), B (fret 2, below ledger line 1), C (fret 3, ledger line 1).
  • String 6 (low E): E (open, below ledger line 3), F (fret 1, ledger line 3), G (fret 3, between ledger lines 2 and 3).
  • Tip for ledger lines: count down by step from C (ledger line 1).
  • The thumb (p) plays strings 4–6, moving down and slightly outward, not hooking upward.
  • Putting all 6 strings together: two-octave C major scale from C on string 5 (introduction only, not to memorise yet).
  • Melodies: a simple bass line, a Carulli-style "bass + treble" exercise.

Lesson flow (60 minutes)

  • 5' — Warm-up, tuning
  • 10' — Review strings 1–3 (teacher names a note, student frets it), watch the homework video
  • 25' — String 4 → 5 → 6, practise reading ledger lines
  • 15' — Bass melody, then a piece with both bass and treble notes
  • 5' — Summary, assign homework

Online teaching notes

  • Bass strings are often "swallowed" by laptop mics → ask the student to bring the guitar closer to the mic or use an external mic.
  • Turn on original sound and switch off noise suppression so low notes aren't lost.
  • The camera should show the right-hand thumb; if not, ask the student to turn the guitar sideways to the camera for a moment.
  • Call-and-response one bar at a time; check whether the student is reading or playing from memory (change the note order).
  • Share the PDF with numbered bars so you can say "play bar 3 again" instead of pointing.
  • Pre-record a video of the teacher playing the melody slowly (60 bpm) and at medium tempo (80 bpm).

Common mistakes

  • Confusing E on string 6 with E on string 4 (both E, but different octaves and staff positions).
  • Miscounting ledger lines → playing on the wrong string.
  • The thumb catching 2 strings at once.
  • Right wrist bent too far when reaching for string 6.

Homework

  • Ledger-line flashcards: 5 minutes a day.
  • Play every first-position note from low E up to high G, saying the names aloud.
  • Practise the 2 melodies in the PDF; send a video of one.

Completion criteria

  • Frets any note on strings 4–6 correctly when the teacher names it
  • Names 6/6 ledger-line notes below the staff correctly
  • Plays an 8-bar bass + treble melody at 60 bpm
