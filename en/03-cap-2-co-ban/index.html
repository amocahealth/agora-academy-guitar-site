Level 2: Foundation

12 sessions: reading treble clef, p-i-m-a arpeggios, 7th/sus chords, the Ballad–Boston–Bolero–Slow rock strumming styles, the first barre chords and the first classical étude.

About this level

  • Level 2 builds on Level 1: from "can play a few chords" to "can read notes and accompany a whole song".
  • The two tracks now diverge more clearly: Classical (co-dien: note reading, right-hand fingers, études) and Accompaniment (dem-hat, Đệm hát (song accompaniment): the strumming styles popular in Vietnam).
  • Lessons marked chung (Core) are required for every student.
  • Lessons from the other track: still worth taking, but the teacher may cut them to 15–20' and use the rest of the time for the student's main track.

Entry requirements

  • Has completed Level 1: Beginner and passed the end-of-level test.
  • Can tune the guitar alone (with an app) in under 3 minutes.
  • Changes smoothly between Em, Am, C, G and D with a metronome at 60 bpm.
  • Can read tab and chord diagrams.
  • Can keep a simple 4/4 pulse with a metronome.

Learning outcomes

  • Read treble-clef notes in first position on all 6 strings.
  • Play rest stroke (apoyando) and free stroke (tirando) with a clear tone.
  • Play p-i-m-a arpeggios in at least 4 patterns.
  • Fret the common 7th and sus chords; understand their "tension – release" function.
  • Accompany in 4 styles: Ballad, Boston, Bolero and Slow rock, at slow to medium tempo.
  • Fret the F and Bm barre chords (not yet perfect, but every string must ring).
  • Play alternating bass accompaniment for open-position chords.
  • Play 1–2 easy classical études (Carulli, Sor, Giuliani).

Suggested practice time

  • Adults / teenagers: 25–40 minutes a day, 5–6 days a week.
  • Children (under 12): 15–20 minutes a day, split into 2 short sessions.
  • Suggested split for a 30-minute practice session:
    • 5' — hand warm-up, tuning
    • 10' — technique (notes, right-hand fingers, chord changes)
    • 10' — main piece (song or étude)
    • 5' — play something you enjoy, relax
  • Always practise with a metronome; record audio/video at least once a week to send to the teacher.

General notes on teaching this level online

  • Level 2 uses a lot of sheet music → prepare a PDF for each session and share your screen; don't hold paper up to the camera.
  • Barre chords and right-hand fingers need 2 camera angles (front view + hand close-up) — remind students to set this up from session 1.
  • Because of latency, never play at the same time as the student: use call-and-response.
  • Every lesson has a demo video/backing track in its Media section for home practice.
