Lesson 6: Bossa Nova

Accompaniment track⏱ 60 minDraft

Steady thumb bass + syncopated chord stabs with i-m-a; maj7, m7 and 7 chords and the ii–V–I progression.

Objectives

  • Students play a basic Bossa Nova pattern: the thumb keeps the bass while i-m-a pluck the chord in syncopation.
  • Students play colour chords: maj7, m7, 7, m7b5 (barre shapes rooted on strings 6 and 5).
  • Students accompany a ii – V – I progression seamlessly at tempo 90 (stretch goal: 100–120).

Preparation

  • Teacher: demonstration video of the pattern slow/fast, PDF of the rhythm notation (bass + chord stabs), basic jazz chord chart.
  • Student: guitar (nylon strings are ideal), metronome; barre chords from lesson 2 secure.

Main content

  • Feel: light and soft, no strumming; everything is played with the fingers.
  • Thumb: bass on beats 1 and 3 (often alternating root – fifth), steady as a heartbeat.
  • i-m-a stabs: pluck the top 3 strings together, landing off the beat (before / after the beat) in a 2-bar pattern.

  • Practise in layers: (1) bass only; (2) stabs only, counting aloud; (3) combined, tempo 60.
  • Chords: Cmaj7, Dm7, Em7, G7, A7, Bm7b5 — mostly barre shapes that don't use string 1 or string 6.
  • Practice progression: Dm7 – G7 – Cmaj7 – A7 (ii – V – I – VI7).
ii – V – I – VI7 in C (jazz voicings, leaving out string 1 or string 6)
  • Reference listening (don't copy the scores): Garota de Ipanema, Corcovado, Desafinado — titles + chord progressions only.
  • Applying it to Vietnamese songs: many nhạc trữ tình (Vietnamese sentimental ballads) and nhạc Trịnh (songs by Trịnh Công Sơn) in 4/4 can be "bossa-fied" — the teacher picks suitable songs from the song list (appendix).

Lesson flow (60 minutes)

  • 5' — Warm-up / greetings, check audio.
  • 10' — Review: transposing, E-shape/A-shape barre chords.
  • 25' — New material: listen to the model, colour chords, separate bass / stab layers.
  • 15' — Guided practice: combine the pattern over ii – V – I, tempo 60 → 80.
  • 5' — Summary, assign homework.

Online teaching notes

  • Syncopation is extremely sensitive to latency → never accompany together. The teacher plays 2 bars, the student repeats them (call-and-response).
  • The student runs their own metronome, placed next to the microphone so the teacher can hear the reference beat.
  • The second camera films the right hand: check that the thumb doesn't get dragged along when i-m-a pluck.
  • Ask the student to record a 1-minute take during the lesson and send the file — checking timing on a recording is more accurate than over the connection.
  • Screen-share the rhythm-notation PDF and use the pointer to show each beat while counting.

Common mistakes

  • Chord stabs landing on the beat → it sounds like a Ballad and loses the Bossa feel.
  • Uneven bass, pulled along by the rhythm of the stabs.
  • Plucking too hard → the lightness is lost.
  • maj7 chords played without the 7th (turning into a plain major chord).

Homework

  • Every day: 5' practising layers separately + 10' combined, tempo 60 → 90.
  • Memorise the 6 colour chords in 2 positions.
  • Apply the Bossa pattern to 1 Vietnamese song chosen by the teacher; write out the chords, no lyrics.
  • Send a 1-minute video accompanying ii – V – I.

Completion criteria

  • Steady bass and correctly syncopated stabs at tempo 90.
  • Changes Dm7 – G7 – Cmaj7 without breaks.
  • Accompanies a complete song in Bossa style.
