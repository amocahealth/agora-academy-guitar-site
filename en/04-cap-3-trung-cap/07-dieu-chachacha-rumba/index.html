Lesson 7: Cha-cha-cha, Rumba and Disco

Accompaniment track⏱ 60 minDraft

Three dance styles common in Vietnamese song accompaniment: Cha-cha-cha, Rumba, Disco — strumming patterns, muting, and choosing a style for a song.

Objectives

  • Students can accompany in 3 styles: Cha-cha-cha, Rumba, Disco.
  • Students use muting (chặn tiếng) with the right and left hands to create a percussive "chop".
  • Students choose a suitable style for a song based on its tempo and feel.

Preparation

  • Teacher: video of the 3 styles (slow + full tempo), PDF of strumming-pattern notation, list of suggested songs (titles + chords only).
  • Student: guitar, metronome, pick (optional, for Disco); barre chords secure.

Main content

  • Cha-cha-cha (4/4, ~110–130 bpm): the "cha – cha – cha" group falls on beat 4 – "and" – 1; pattern: bass – mute – bass – mute – "cha cha cha".

  • Muting technique: the right hand drops the edge of the palm onto the strings straight after the strum; the left hand releases pressure without leaving the strings.
  • E-shape/A-shape barre chords make muting easier than open chords.
  • Choosing a style: medium tempo + cheerful → Cha-cha-cha; slower, sentimental → Rumba; fast, energetic → Disco.
  • Reference listening: Cherry Pink and Apple Blossom White (cha-cha-cha), Bésame Mucho (usually accompanied as Rumba/Bolero) — titles + chord progressions only.
  • Vietnamese songs: the teacher chooses from the song list (appendix) according to the student's taste.

Lesson flow (60 minutes)

  • 5' — Warm-up / greetings, check audio.
  • 10' — Review: Bossa Nova pattern, video feedback.
  • 25' — New material: listen to and tell apart the 3 styles; Cha-cha-cha → Rumba → Disco patterns.
  • 15' — Guided practice: each style over Am – Dm – G – C, slow tempo.
  • 5' — Summary, assign homework.

Online teaching notes

  • Zoom easily treats percussive muted strums as noise and cuts them → turn on "Original sound for musicians" and turn off noise suppression.
  • "Guess the style" exercise: the teacher plays 4 bars, the student types the style name in the chat → not affected by latency.
  • Call-and-response one 1-bar pattern at a time; never strum together.
  • The second camera (or a turned main camera) films the right hand to show the palm-muting motion.
  • Students record 30 seconds of each style with a metronome and send it in — timing is easier to check on a recording.

Common mistakes

  • Cha-cha-cha and Rumba sound the same because the accents aren't placed correctly.
  • Left-hand muting by lifting the fingers right off → open strings ring through.
  • Disco strummed too hard, rushing faster and faster.
  • Choosing a style that doesn't suit the song's tempo.

Homework

  • 5'/day on each style over Am – Dm – G – C and C – Am – F – G.
  • Pick 1 song for each style; write down the chords and the chosen style (no lyrics).
  • Send 1 short video of yourself singing + accompanying a song in a style of your choice.

Completion criteria

  • Plays the patterns of all 3 styles correctly with a metronome.
  • Identifies the 3 styles when the teacher plays them (3/3).
  • Clean muting, with no open strings ringing through.
