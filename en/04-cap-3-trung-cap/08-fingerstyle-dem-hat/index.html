Level 3: Intermediate · Lesson 8/12

Lesson 8: Fingerstyle accompaniment — Travis picking

Accompaniment track⏱ 60 minDraft

Thumb alternating the bass while the fingers fill in the off-beats; from Travis accompaniment to a fingerstyle intro with a melody.

Objectives

  • Students play Travis picking: the thumb alternates steadily between 2 bass strings on every beat.
  • Students add i and m on the "and" beats without the thumb losing its way.
  • Students layer a simple melody on top of the bass (a fingerstyle intro).

Preparation

  • Teacher: teacher-written Travis exercise PDF/tab (chord progressions, no copyrighted tabs), close-up video of the right hand.
  • Student: guitar, metronome; lesson 5 (chord progressions) and lesson 2 (barre chords) secure.

Main content

  • Thumb (p): alternating bass across 4 beats — root – string 4 – alternate bass – string 4 (e.g. C: string 5 – string 4 – string 6 – string 4).
  • Step 1: thumb only, automatic enough to count aloud while playing.
  • Step 2: add i (string 2) on the "and" of beat 2 and m (string 1) on the "and" of beat 3.
  • Step 3: a pinch of p + m on beat 1.
  • Apply it to C – Am – F – G and G – Em – C – D.
  • Variation: lightly mute the bass with the heel of the right hand (palm mute) for a tight, folk-style sound.
  • Melody over the bass: melody notes replace the i/m notes, landing on the beat or syncopated.
  • Reference listening (don't copy tabs): Dust in the Wind (Kansas) and folk accompaniments using a similar picking pattern.
  • Applying it to slow Vietnamese songs in 4/4: fingerstyle intro → switch to strumming (Ballad) in the chorus to build the climax.

Lesson flow (60 minutes)

  • 5' — Warm-up / greetings, check audio, check the right-hand camera.
  • 10' — Review: the 3 styles Cha-cha-cha / Rumba / Disco.
  • 25' — New material: Travis steps 1 → 2 → 3, each step secure before adding the next.
  • 15' — Guided practice: Travis over a 4-chord progression, try layering a 4-bar melody.
  • 5' — Summary, assign homework.

Online teaching notes

  • A second camera filming the right hand from above (the player's point of view) is practically essential — the main camera can't show the thumb.
  • Call-and-response one bar at a time; the teacher says "thumb only" / "add i" clearly before each turn, because hand signals are hard to see on video.
  • Soft, low bass notes are easily compressed away by Zoom → turn on "Original sound for musicians" and turn off automatic microphone volume adjustment.
  • The student records one take with a metronome after each step; the teacher listens to the recording to check whether the bass is steady.
  • Screen-share the exercise tab, colouring thumb notes and i/m notes differently.

Common mistakes

  • The thumb "follows" i/m → the bass loses its evenness and the timing drifts.
  • Bass too loud, drowning the melody (or vice versa) — not yet balanced.
  • Changing chords but forgetting to change the root bass string (F root on string 4, C root on string 5, G root on string 6).
  • Speeding up too early, before step 1 is automatic.

Homework

  • Every day: 5' thumb only + 10' full Travis, tempo 60 → 90.
  • Accompany a Vietnamese song of your choice with Travis picking (write out the chords, no lyrics).
  • Compose a 4–8 bar intro: a single-line melody over a Travis pattern.
  • Send a video that shows the right hand.

Completion criteria

  • Steady thumb throughout 16 bars at tempo 80.
  • Full Travis over a 4-chord progression, changing chords without breaks.
  • Sings + accompanies a complete section of a song with Travis picking.
