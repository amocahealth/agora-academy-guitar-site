Level 3: Intermediate · Lesson 12/12

Lesson 12: Performing on camera — end-of-Level 3 test

Core⏱ 60 minDraft

End-of-level test via a single-take performance video: programme by track, recording requirements and a 20-point marking rubric.

Objectives

  • Students prepare and record a complete performance video themselves (a single continuous take, no editing).
  • Students get used to the pressure of the "red recording light" and handle mistakes without stopping.
  • The teacher assesses with a common rubric and decides on progression to Level 4.

Preparation

  • Teacher: the rubric (below) as a PDF; send the programme and recording requirements 1–2 weeks in advance.
  • Student: phone/camera + stand, a quiet room, light coming from in front; guitar tuned accurately.

Main content

  • Programme — Classical track:
    • 1 major scale + 1 minor scale over 2 octaves (key chosen by the teacher).
    • Romance Anónimo complete, or Lágrima / Adelita.
  • Programme — Accompaniment track:
    • Sing + accompany 1 song in a Latin style (Bossa Nova, Cha-cha-cha or Rumba), using barre chords.
    • 1 Travis-picking fingerstyle accompaniment (may include a self-composed intro); state the key and capo position before playing.
  • Recording requirements:
    • One continuous take; the frame shows both hands and the neck.
    • A short introduction before playing: name, piece title, composer.
    • Record with the phone microphone or an external mic; no effects, no audio editing.
    • Submit the original file (not compressed via Zalo/Messenger) through Google Drive or the class's designated channel.
  • The 60-minute session is used for: a dress rehearsal, reviewing the draft video, final feedback, or recording live if the student wishes.

Marking rubric (20 points)

Criterion4 — Good2 — Pass0–1 — Not yet
Accuracy (notes, chords, fingering)Almost no mistakesSmall mistakes, flow unaffectedMany mistakes, stops
Rhythm and tempoSteady, true to the styleSometimes rushes/dragsLoses the beat
Sound qualityClear tone, barres ring, no buzzingA few buzzing/muted spotsLots of buzzing
Musicality (dynamics, melody projection, groove)Clear musical intentPresent but inconsistentMonotonous, no dynamics
On-camera presenceConfident, handles mistakes without stoppingA bit tense but finishesStops / restarts midway
  • Passing Level 3: ≥ 14/20 and no criterion below 2.
  • 10–13 points: re-record after 2 weeks, focusing on the weak criteria.
  • ≥ 14 points but a criterion below 2: not yet passed — re-record after 2 weeks, focusing on that criterion; passed when it reaches ≥ 2 and the total stays ≥ 14.
  • Below 10 points: not yet passed — 3–4 weeks of review following the teacher's plan (the Level 3 lessons linked to the weak criteria), then re-record the whole programme.
  • A score of 3 is used for the level between "Good" and "Pass".

Lesson flow (60 minutes)

  • 5' — Warm-up / greetings, check audio and the recording frame.
  • 10' — Review the draft video (from lesson 11 or a draft sent in advance), feedback against the rubric.
  • 25' — Dress rehearsal: play the whole programme as if recording for real.
  • 15' — Fix the 2–3 weakest points; practise "don't stop when you make a mistake".
  • 5' — Set the video deadline; explain how marking and feedback work.

Online teaching notes

  • The self-recorded video is the main evidence for marking — far better quality than audio over Zoom.
  • If recording live during the session: the student records on a phone in parallel (Zoom is only for the teacher to watch), with "Original sound for musicians" on.
  • Check the framing with a test photo before the real take; a second camera on the right hand is a plus, not a requirement.
  • Send written feedback for each criterion, with timestamps (min:sec) in the video.
  • Stay encouraging: many Vietnamese students are shy about filming themselves — let them record as many drafts as they like and submit only the best.

Common mistakes

  • Too many takes → tired hands, and the last take is worse than the first.
  • Microphone too close → distortion in the f passages.
  • The frame cuts off the neck or the right hand.
  • Stopping to fix a mistake mid-piece instead of carrying on.

Homework

  • Record at least 2 drafts during the week and self-assess with the rubric.
  • Submit the official video on time.
  • After receiving the result: write down 3 things to improve in Level 4.

Completion criteria

  • Video submitted meeting the recording requirements.
  • Scores ≥ 14/20, with no criterion below 2.
  • Has received and read the teacher's feedback.
