Level 3: Intermediate

12 sessions: positions, movable barre chords, legato, scales, applied harmony, Latin styles, fingerstyle, dynamics and tone colour, and Tárrega / Romance.

About this level

  • Level 3 is the bridge from "able to play" to "playing musically".
  • Students leave first position and use the whole fretboard.
  • Classical track: first complete concert pieces (Tárrega, Romance Anónimo).
  • Accompaniment track: enough styles and skills to accompany most popular Vietnamese songs on their own (đệm hát — song accompaniment).
  • The core material (positions, barre chords, legato, scales) is compulsory for both tracks.

Entry requirements

  • Completed Level 2 (passed the end-of-Level 2 test), or an equivalent placement assessment.
  • Can play F and Bm barre chords and change chords without breaking the beat at tempo 70.
  • Reads treble-clef notation in first position (classical), or reads tab + chord diagrams quickly (accompaniment).
  • Plays p-i-m-a arpeggios evenly with a metronome.
  • Can accompany in at least 2 styles (Ballad, Boston, Bolero or Slow Rock).

Learning outcomes

  • Knows the note names on all 6 strings up to fret 12; plays in positions II, V and VII.
  • Plays movable E-shape and A-shape barre chords in any position.
  • Plays legato (hammer-on, pull-off, slide) cleanly.
  • Plays 2-octave major / minor scales in several common keys.
  • (Accompaniment) Transposes, uses a capo, accompanies in Bossa Nova, Cha-cha-cha, Rumba, Disco and Travis-picking fingerstyle.
  • (Classical) Controls dynamics and tone colour; plays Lágrima or Adelita and Romance Anónimo in full.
  • Records a video performance of a complete piece on their own.

  • Classical-track students still take session 5 in a shortened form (reading chord progressions, transposing).
  • Accompaniment-track students take session 1 in a shortened form: note names + positions V/VII (for barre chords and simple solos).
  • A session from the other track may be replaced with an extra session from the student's own track — the teacher decides.

Suggested practice time

  • At least 30–45 minutes/day, 5 days/week.
  • Split each practice session: 10' technique (scales, legato) · 20' current piece · 10' review old material / metronome work.
  • Each week, send at least 1 practice video (1–2 minutes) to the class group for teacher feedback.
  • Expected length of the level: 12 sessions, about 3–4 months (1 session/week).

Online setup for this level

  • Level 3 requires hearing tone colour and dynamics clearly → turn on "Original sound for musicians" (Zoom) on both sides.
  • A second camera (phone) is recommended, filming the right hand or the neck from fret V upwards.
  • Share the PDF score on screen before each session; students print it in advance or open it on a tablet.
