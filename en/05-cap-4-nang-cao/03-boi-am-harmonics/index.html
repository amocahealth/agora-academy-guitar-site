Lesson 3: Harmonics

Core⏱ 60 minDraft

Natural harmonics at frets 12, 7 and 5 and right-hand artificial harmonics; notation, reading and applications.

Objectives

  • Students can tell natural harmonics from artificial harmonics.
  • Play clear natural harmonics at frets 12, 7 and 5 on all 6 strings.
  • Play artificial harmonics (right hand touching 12 frets above the fretted note) for a short melody.
  • Read harmonic notation in standard notation and tab (Harm., ◇, "Arm.", "AH").

Preparation

  • Teacher: a fretboard diagram marking the harmonic points (frets 12, 7, 5, 4, 3.2); a tuner app that shows pitch.
  • Teacher: a short Vietnamese folk melody (e.g. the first phrase of a well-known folk song) arranged in artificial harmonics.
  • Student: guitar in standard tuning, a quiet room, tuner app.

Main content

  • Principle: lightly touching the string at a division point (1/2, 1/3, 1/4) cancels the fundamental and keeps an overtone.
  • Natural harmonics:
    • Fret 12 → one octave above the open string.
    • Fret 7 (and 19) → a twelfth (octave + fifth).
    • Fret 5 (and 24) → two octaves.
    • Fret 4 / 9 → two octaves + a major third (harder, needs precise placement).
  • Left-hand technique: touch directly over the fret wire (not between frets), lift right after plucking.
  • Artificial harmonics:
    • Left hand frets the note normally.
    • Right-hand i lightly touches the point 12 frets above the fretted note; a (or p) plucks.
    • Variant: p plucks + a touches, when combining with a bass line.
  • Applications:
    • Tuning with harmonics at frets 5–7 (review of string-matching).
    • Classical track: harmonic endings in works by Tárrega and Sor.
    • Accompaniment track: intros/endings with fret-12 harmonic chords (Em, "bells" at frets 12–7).

Lesson flow (60 minutes)

  • 5' — Warm-up / greetings, sound check
  • 10' — Review rasgueado, listen to homework
  • 25' — Natural harmonics at frets 12 → 7 → 5; artificial harmonics on strings 1–3
  • 15' — Guided practice: a short melody in artificial harmonics
  • 5' — Summary, assign homework

Online teaching notes

  • Harmonics are quiet and high-pitched → Zoom/Meet noise suppression cuts them out; "Original sound" on and noise suppression off is mandatory.
  • The student should put the mic close (20–30 cm) and aim it at the 12th fret when practising harmonics.
  • Use the tuner app as the "referee": if it shows the right harmonic pitch, it's correct — the teacher doesn't need to hear it perfectly over the network.
  • Screen-share the fretboard diagram with the harmonic points throughout the new-material section.
  • Close-up camera on the left hand to check touch placement (directly over the fret).

Common mistakes

  • Pressing the string down to the fret → a fretted note instead of a harmonic.
  • Touching between frets instead of over the fret → dull sound.
  • Lifting the left hand too early or too late.
  • Artificial harmonics: i touches at the wrong distance after changing position (forgetting the +12 frets).
  • Plucking too close to the touching finger → muffled; pluck 3–5 cm away from the touch point.

Homework

  • Natural harmonics: play frets 12, 7, 5 on all 6 strings in turn, 2 rounds a day.
  • Tune with harmonics at frets 5–7 every practice session.
  • Artificial harmonics: one-octave C major scale on strings 2–3.
  • Send a recording of the short melody in artificial harmonics.

Completion criteria

  • Clear natural harmonics at frets 12, 7, 5 on all 6 strings.
  • One-octave C major scale in artificial harmonics, at least 7/8 notes clear.
  • Reads harmonic notation correctly in 3 notation/tab examples.
  • Tunes with harmonics without a tuner (checked afterwards with a tuner).
