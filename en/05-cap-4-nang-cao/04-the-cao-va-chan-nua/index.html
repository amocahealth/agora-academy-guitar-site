Lesson 4: High positions and half barre

Classical track⏱ 60 minDraft

Reading and playing in positions V–XII, smooth position shifts, the half barre and its notation in classical scores.

Objectives

  • Students quickly find notes from the staff in positions V, VII, IX and XII.
  • Shift positions smoothly using a guide finger, without breaking the sound.
  • Play a clean half barre in different positions.
  • Read position and barre markings correctly: Roman numerals, "C", "½C", "CII", "BV"…

Preparation

  • Teacher: a public-domain etude using high positions, e.g. Carcassi Op. 60 or Sor Op. 31/35 (choose one with ½C); mark the shift points in advance.
  • Teacher: a close-up camera on the neck from the player's side (left-hand fingers clearly visible).
  • Student: printed score or PDF; pencil for fingerings; footstool/raised seat for correct classical posture.

Main content

  • Posture review: high positions need the neck angled higher, a straight left wrist and a freely hanging elbow.
  • Fretboard map:
    • Landmark notes: frets V, VII, XII on strings 1, 2, 6.
    • Rule: position = the fret where finger 1 sits.
  • Shifting:
    • Guide finger: slide lightly along the string when the same finger is used before and after.
    • Shift during an open string (to buy time for the jump).
    • Look ahead to the destination, not at the hand that is leaving.
  • Half barre:
    • Finger 1 covers only the top 2–4 strings; gentle finger joint, don't press with the whole finger.
    • Force comes from arm weight pulling back, not from squeezing with the thumb.
    • Common markings: ½CV, C½ V, or a horizontal line with a Roman numeral.
  • High positions on strings 1–2: small frets, so fingers need to be curved and placed closer to the fret.

Lesson flow (60 minutes)

  • 5' — Warm-up / greetings, sound check
  • 10' — Review harmonics; use the fret-12 harmonic as the landmark for position XII
  • 25' — Fretboard map, shifting technique, half barre
  • 15' — Guided practice: the chosen etude, first 8–16 bars
  • 5' — Summary, assign homework

Online teaching notes

  • Webcams often mirror the image → the teacher labels "string 1 is at the bottom" on screen or turns off mirroring.
  • Screen-share the PDF score and annotate position numbers and ½C points live.
  • Ask the student to put a second phone at the headstock end, looking down the neck → finger curvature and half barre are visible.
  • When checking a half barre: the student plucks each string slowly one at a time and the teacher listens for buzzing — much easier to hear over the network than a full strummed chord.
  • Send the marked-up etude PDF before the lesson so the student can print it.

Common mistakes

  • Squeezing too hard with the thumb when barring → fatigue, shaky hand.
  • Barring too many strings → unwanted strings get muted.
  • Lifting the finger off the string when shifting → the melody "breaks".
  • Staring at the score, not remembering positions → memorise 4-bar chunks.
  • Wrist bent too far in positions IX–XII.

Homework

  • C major and A minor scales in positions V and VII (2 octaves).
  • Shifting exercise: the same E on strings 1, 2, 3 (open position, V, IX).
  • The chosen etude: finish the first 16 bars, with fingerings and positions written in.
  • Send a video of the 16 bars, filmed so the left hand is visible.

Completion criteria

  • Names any note in positions V and VII within 3 seconds.
  • Shifts in the etude without breaking the sound.
  • Half barre in positions II, V, VII clean string by string.
  • Reads every position/barre marking in the etude correctly.
