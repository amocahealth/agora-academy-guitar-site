Level 4: Advanced

12 advanced sessions: tremolo, rasgueado, harmonics, major works, solo arranging, accompanying singers, improvisation, band playing and a graduation recital.

Introduction

  • The final level of the program: from "being able to play" to "performing and making your own music".
  • Draws on the teacher's experience both as a classical concert performer and as a stage/band musician.
  • Each session is 60 minutes, taught online 1-to-1 or in small groups (max. 4 students).
  • Students pick a main track (Classical or Accompaniment — Đệm hát, song accompaniment) but still take the chung (Core) lessons.

Entry requirements

  • Completed Level 3: Intermediate (including Lesson 12 — Performing on camera).
  • Full barre chords (F, Bm, movable barre shapes) ring clean; chord changes without breaking the beat at 80 bpm.
  • Major/minor scales over at least 2 octaves in positions I–V.
  • Classical track: can play a complete Tárrega piece or Romance (Anónimo).
  • Accompaniment track: confident in at least 4 styles (ballad, bolero, bossa nova, chachacha/rumba).
  • Practises regularly, at least 5 sessions a week of 45 minutes or more.

Learning outcomes

  • Plays tremolo, rasgueado, and natural and artificial harmonics at performance level.
  • Completes at least one advanced piece (Classical track) or one solo arrangement of a Vietnamese song (both tracks).
  • Accompanies a singer: intro, ending, changing key, following the singer.
  • Basic improvisation on the pentatonic and harmonic minor scales.
  • Understands the guitar's role in a band and the rules of group rehearsal.
  • Records audio/video of a performance at home to a decent standard.
  • Performs a 15–20-minute graduation recital, assessed with a rubric.

Notes for teachers

  • Sessions 5 and 7 often need 2 real sessions — split them with an extra session if the student needs it.
  • From session 9 on, ask students to send a homework video every week (see Appendix — Assessment form template).
  • Start choosing the recital program in session 5 so there are at least 6 weeks to prepare.
