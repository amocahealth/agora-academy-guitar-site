How to read the tables

  • The Italian/Spanish column gives the term printed in classical sheet music (usually Italian) or the Spanish name of a guitar technique.
  • "—" means there is no common Italian/Spanish term; use the English one.
  • The first time a term appears in a lesson, write it in Vietnamese with the English/Italian term in brackets.
  • Abbreviations: (It.) = Italian, (Sp.) = Spanish.
