Copyright notes

  • The list gives only titles and composers; this material contains no lyrics, tabs or sheet music of songs still under copyright.
  • In class, use only the title + chord progression; students find the lyrics themselves from legal sources.
  • Public performances / monetised videos require a copyright check (in Vietnam: the Vietnam Center for Protection of Music Copyright — VCPMC).
  • Folk songs and classical works marked "public domain" can be used freely from sheet music — choose a public-domain edition/version.
  • The teacher should double-check composer credits before putting them into official material; add songs as students request them.

Key

  • Track: chung (Core) · co-dien (Classical) · dem-hat (Accompaniment).
  • Level: 1 Beginner · 2 Foundation · 3 Intermediate · 4 Advanced.
  • Vietnamese titles are kept as written; English glosses in brackets are approximate.

Material for improvisation and band playing (Level 4)

  • Am – Dm – Am – E7 progression (slow bolero): improvising with the harmonic minor.
  • Am – G – F – E progression (rumba / flamenco-pop): pentatonic + the note G#.
  • ii – V – I progression in C and F: extended chords, fills.
  • I – vi – IV – V progression (pop ballad): practising accompaniment/lead roles in a band.
