Online guitar curriculum

Agora Academy · Guitar

Online guitar curriculum: classical and đệm hát (singing with guitar accompaniment), from first steps to advanced.

Welcome

  • Welcome to the online guitar programme of Agora Academy.
  • The teacher is a professional classical guitarist who has played in many bands.
  • Lessons run live on Zoom / Google Meet; students practise at home and send homework videos via Zalo (Vietnam's main messaging app).
  • Goal: correct technique from day one, and playing music you love as early as possible.

Who the course is for

  • Teens and adults who have never played an instrument.
  • Self-taught players (from YouTube, friends) who want to relearn properly.
  • Children (from about 8 years old) learning with a parent's support.
  • People who want to play classical guitar: reading notation, fingerstyle technique, solo pieces.
  • People who want to play đệm hát (accompany singing): pop, ballad, bolero, Trịnh Công Sơn songs… to sing with family and friends.

A 4-level pathway

LevelNameDurationAfter this level, students can
1Beginner (Nhập môn)12 sessions (~3 months)Hold the guitar with correct posture, play basic chords, strum or play a simple melody
2Foundation (Cơ bản)12 sessions (~3 months)Read treble-clef notation, or accompany a whole song in ballad or bolero style; play barre chords
3Intermediate (Trung cấp)12 sessions (~3 months)Play in several positions, a Tárrega piece, bossa nova, fingerstyle
4Advanced (Nâng cao)12 sessions (~3 months)Tremolo, accompanying a singer, arranging solos of Vietnamese songs, graduation recital

Two tracks

TrackForTypical content
Core (chung)All studentsPosture, tuning, chords, rhythm, left- / right-hand technique
Classical track (co-dien)Students who enjoy solo playing, nylon-string guitarReading notation, apoyando / tirando, études, works by Tárrega, Sor, Carulli
Accompaniment track (dem-hat)Students who want to play and singBallad, bolero, bossa nova, rumba, accompanying a singer
  • Level 1 is shared by everyone, with a "taster" of both tracks.
  • From Level 2, students choose a main track; they may switch at the end of each level.

A typical week

  • 1–2 online sessions of 60 minutes each (Zoom or Google Meet).
  • 15–30 minutes of home practice a day, following the assigned homework.
  • A short phone video (30 seconds – 2 minutes) sent via Zalo or Google Drive.
  • The teacher replies with time-stamped comments, e.g. "0:45 — left wrist slightly bent".
  • The next session starts by reviewing the homework together and fixing mistakes.

Where to start

  • Picking up a guitar for the first time: start with Basic notes on each string and keep the chord chart next to you while practising.
  • New students: read the Level pathway, then book a placement assessment.
  • Teachers: read the Online teaching guide before your first lesson.
